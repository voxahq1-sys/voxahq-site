How it works

Paste or upload

One ad, or 200 at once. Copy in the box, the creative image for the text inside it, and the landing page URL. No account needed to try it.

Get a verdict

Red, amber or green, with the exact line that tripped a rule, the reason behind it, and a rewrite that keeps the offer. Landing pages get their own check: status, tracking parameters and any risky claim on the page.

Approve and log it

Clear the safe ones, fix the rest, and every decision is recorded with a name and a timestamp. Export the log when a client or a platform asks who signed off.

What is inside

Bulk check

Up to 200 ads in one pass. Each row gets a verdict and its top issue. Download the results as CSV.

Image text

Upload the creative and the tool reads the words inside it, because half the claims hide in the picture, not the copy.

Landing page check

Fetches the page, flags the claims on it, checks the tracking parameters, and warns when the page does not match the ad.

Claims library

Add the phrases your legal team already cleared. They pass instead of blocking, so the same sentence is not argued twice.

Approvals with a log

Approve or reject with your name on it. Every entry is timestamped and exportable for the client file.

API

One POST returns a verdict per ad, so a launcher can gate every push without a human in the middle.

Who it is for

  • Media buyers running health, supplement and telehealth offers at volume, where one policy slip costs an account.
  • Agencies that need a client sign-off before a launch, on the record, without a WhatsApp thread.
  • Launchers and platforms that want a policy gate in front of the push button.

Preflight does not guarantee approval. Platform review is partly automated judgement. It catches the written rules, which is where most rejections start, and it keeps the record of what was cleared and by whom.

Questions

Does it replace a compliance team?

No. It removes the repetitive first pass. A human still signs off, and the log records who did.

Which platforms does it cover?

Meta health and wellness, TikTok weight management and body image, and Google healthcare and financial services. Platform limits such as headline length are checked too.

What happens to my ads?

The checker runs in your browser. Nothing is stored on the server, and the landing page is fetched once only if you enter a URL.

Can it be wrong?

Yes, in one direction: it can pass something a reviewer rejects, because platform review includes automated judgement. It is built to be quiet on ads that are fine, which is why the test set includes 52 live ads with zero false alarms.

Can I add my own rules?

On the Agency plan, yes. Send the phrase or the claim type and it goes into your rule set, kept separate from the shared list.
