Affiliate Disclosure

Home/Affiliate Disclosure

Last updated: 31 October 2026

VOXA is an independent publication about AI tools and automation. Most of what we publish carries no affiliate links at all. Some does, and this page explains exactly where, how it is marked, and what it does not change.

Where affiliate links appear

Only in the section. News, guides and notes do not contain affiliate links. If that ever changes, this page changes first and the top of the article says so.

How they are marked

Every page with an affiliate link carries a disclosure block before the first paragraph, so you see it before you read the recommendation. Links that earn a commission are marked rel="sponsored nofollow" in the HTML, and the sentence next to the link says what we earn.

What we earn

When you buy a product through an affiliate link on a page that carries one, the seller pays us a commission. The price you pay is the same, and any discount code we publish works the same way. We do not get paid for a review, for a ranking, or for a mention.

What a commission does not buy

  • No paid verdicts. A vendor cannot pay to be called good, and cannot pay to have a limitation removed from a page.
  • No hidden testing status. Each page with an affiliate link says whether the tool was tested end to end, only imported and read, or not tested at all. If we have not run it, we say that on the page.
  • No removal of competitors. If a free or cheaper tool does the job better, the page says so, even if it earns us nothing.

Free products and review copies

Vendors sometimes provide a free licence or a review copy so we can look at the product. Where that happens, the page states it. A free copy never comes with a promise to publish, and it never comes with edit rights over what we write.

Advertising is separate

The site also runs display advertising, including Google AdSense. Advertising is sold by the network, not by us, and it has no effect on what we write or which tools we cover. See our editorial policy for how verdicts are kept apart from ads.

Questions

If a link looks wrong, or a disclosure is missing on a page that should have one, tell us through the contact page and we will fix it.
