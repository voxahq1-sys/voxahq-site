Ten seats, one machine

We opened in 2019 with a second hand lever machine and a fridge that hummed too loudly. The idea has not changed: buy small lots, roast them properly, and charge enough to pay the people making it.

Every coffee on the bar comes from a farm we can name, and the roastery is nine minutes away. If something is not right, tell us and we will make it again.
