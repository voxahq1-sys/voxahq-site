Why the fee is this low

Because the research is already done. The prices in our guides are checked by hand, so most audits are a matter of matching your actual usage to numbers we already hold. The fee covers the matching and the write-up, not a discovery project.

Who it is for

Small teams paying for three to ten AI or automation tools, where nobody is quite sure what the total is each month, or why the bill moved. If you are spending under $100 a month in total, the audit will not pay for itself. Read the guides instead, they are free.
