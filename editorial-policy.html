Editorial policy

Home/Editorial policy

Short version: the site runs on display advertising, nobody can buy a verdict, and there are no affiliate links anywhere on VOXA.

How the site is funded

Display advertising, served by Google. That is the whole business model. There is no paid membership, no sponsored section and no lead selling.

What we do not do

  • No affiliate links. We do not earn a commission if you sign up for anything. Every link goes to the product's own page or to a source we are citing.
  • No paid reviews. A vendor cannot buy a guide, a placement in a comparison table or a better verdict.
  • No free-product deals for coverage. If we ever accept early access or a free account, the page says so in plain words.
  • No "best of" slots for sale. If a tool is first in a list, it is first because it suited the job at the price.

Ads and verdicts stay apart

Ads are placed automatically by the ad system, not by us, and they can appear next to any guide. An ad on a page is not an endorsement and does not change what the page says. If an ad ever looks like a VOXA recommendation, tell us and we will look at the placement.

Updates

AI pricing changes every few weeks. Every guide shows the date we last checked the numbers. When a price or a limit changes, we update the page, change that date and, where the change matters, say what changed in the guide itself.

Corrections

If something is wrong, email voxa.hq1@gmail.com with the page address. We fix the page rather than delete it, and we do not quietly rewrite history.

Who writes this

VOXA Editorial, a small desk that publishes under the VOXA name. You can see how the work is done on the how we check page, and reach us through the contact page.
