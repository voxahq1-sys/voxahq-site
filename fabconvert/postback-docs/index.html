Postback setup

One URL, five fields. Your network fires it on a sale, FabConvert stores it against your click id, and the ledger subtracts spend to show net profit.

The URL

https://voxahq.in/pb?w=YOUR-WS&t=YOUR-TOKEN&cid={clickid}&payout={payout}&offer={offer}&txid={transaction_id}&status=sale

Both w and t are on the ledger page once you sign in, inside a ready made URL you can copy in one click. A call without t is refused on purpose: it is what stops someone else writing sales into your ledger.

Fields

wYour workspace key. Required.
tYour postback token. Required, and different for every account. Copy the whole URL from the ledger page rather than typing this by hand.
cidClick id passed to the offer. Also accepts clickid or sub1. Required.
payoutWhat you earned. Also accepts amount. Numbers only.
offerOffer or product name. Optional.
txidTransaction id. Recommended: a repeat fire updates the same record instead of counting twice.
statussale (default), refund, chargeback, or lead. Refund, chargeback, reversal and cancel are all treated as a reversal and subtracted from revenue.

Duplicates and refunds

Same txid twice means one record, not two. Without a txid the fallback is click id plus payout, which catches most repeats but not all.

A refund is a second call with the same txid and status=refund. The ledger subtracts it and counts it separately.

Not every network sends refund postbacks. Where it does not, the ledger cannot know, and the profit stays optimistic. Send it if the network allows it.

ClickBank

In ClickBank open Vendor Settings, Integrated Postback, or on the affiliate side Reporting, Postback URL.

https://voxahq.in/pb?w=YOUR-WS&cid={ctid}&payout={amount}&offer={item}&txid={receipt}&status=sale

ClickBank uses {ctid} for the tracking id and {receipt} for the order id.

Digistore24

Settings, Postback-URL:

https://voxahq.in/pb?w=YOUR-WS&cid=[tracking_id]&payout=[amount]&offer=[product_name]&txid=[order_id]&status=sale

BuyGoods and MaxWeb

Global postback in the affiliate profile, with the macros that network lists:

https://voxahq.in/pb?w=YOUR-WS&cid={sub1}&payout={payout}&offer={offer_id}&txid={transaction_id}&status=sale

Voluum and RedTrack

Send the postback to the tracker first, then let it forward to FabConvert on conversion. Keep the same click id on both hops or the ledger cannot match the sale to the click.

https://voxahq.in/pb?w=YOUR-WS&cid={clickid}&payout={payout}&offer={campaign}&txid={conversion_id}&status=sale

Testing

On the ledger page press Send a test sale. It fires a random click id and the row should appear within a minute. If that works, your URL is right and only the network side is left.

A new entry can take up to a minute to appear, because storage replicates across regions. The page retries by itself.

Reading the ledger

RevenueSales minus refunds and chargebacks, from postbacks only.
SpendEntries you added by hand.
Net profitRevenue minus spend.
ROIRevenue minus spend, divided by spend.
Revenue per conversionRevenue divided by number of sales.
