How it works right now

Payments are not connected yet

There is no card field on this site. A paid plan is switched on by hand after a short email, so nothing can be charged by accident. When the payment rail is live these buttons will point at it.

What happens after the 3 day trial

Nothing is charged. New checks stop until a plan is chosen, and your rules, landers and ledger stay where they are.

Can I move between plans

Yes, in either direction, at the start of a month. Nothing is deleted when you move down.

Is there a refund

Inside the first 14 days of a paid plan, on one reply to any email from us. After that it is our discretion and we answer in one reply. Nothing here limits a right you have under the law where you live.
