How billing works right now

Payments are not connected yet

There is no card field on this site. A paid plan is switched on by hand after a short email, so nothing can be charged by accident. When the payment rail is live, the two paid buttons will point at it and this note will disappear.

Do refused checks count?

No. A check that the server refuses because you hit the limit is not counted, and the counter resets on the first of the month.

What happens if I cancel?

The plan stays active until the end of the period you already paid for, then drops to free. Nothing is deleted, so your rules and your approval log are still there.

Is there a refund?

Yes, inside the first 14 days of a paid plan, on one reply to any email from us. After that it is our discretion and we answer in one reply. Nothing here limits a right you have under the law where you live.

Start with the free plan

50 checks a month, no card, the same rules the paid plans use. If your copy passes, you will know in about two seconds.
