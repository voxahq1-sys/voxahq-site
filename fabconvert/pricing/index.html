Questions about the plans

Q1

What counts as a check?

One ad, once. A bulk run of 50 ads counts as 50. A check refused because you hit the limit does not count against you.

Q2

Do postbacks cost anything?

No. Postbacks, spend entries and the profit ledger are unlimited on every plan, including free.

Q3

Can I change plan later?

Yes. Limits apply from the month you change, and nothing is pro-rated. Write to us and we switch it.

Q4

Is there an annual price?

Not yet. When billing is connected there will be two months free on a yearly plan.

Q5

What if my network does not send refunds?

Then the ledger cannot know, and profit stays optimistic. Ask the network to enable the refund postback; the setup page shows how.
