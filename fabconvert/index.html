Three features, one account

Most buyers pay for a checker, a page builder, a tracker and a spreadsheet. This is those four in one place, and the three here talk to each other.

Feature 1

Compliance check

Paste the copy, add the creative text, add the landing page. A verdict before spend, with the exact line that gets flagged and a rewrite that keeps the offer.

  • 89 rules: health, finance, telehealth, limits
  • One click rewrites the lines it flags
  • Your own rules, learned from your rejections
  • Bulk checks, claims library, approval log
Open the checker
Feature 2

Lander builder

The page the ad points to, in four templates, with up to three variants and a split weight. Publish on your own link with your pixel and click id in place.

  • Advertorial, quiz, listicle, comparison
  • Image upload, hosted for you
  • Clicks and sales counted per variant
Build a lander
Feature 3

Profit tracking

One postback URL from your network. Add what you spent. Net profit, ROI and revenue per conversion, with refunds subtracted as they arrive.

  • Any network that fires a postback
  • Duplicates deduped by transaction id
  • Sale and refund emails
Open the ledger

Start to finish

Four steps, and the last one is why the first three matter.

01

See it

Paste the ad copy, add the creative text and the landing page URL.

02

Check it

89 rules run server side. Red, amber or green, with the reason and the rewrite. The lander gets its own check: status, tracking, claims, and whether it matches the ad.

03

Build it

Pick a template, write the variants, upload the image, drop the pixel in. Publish on your own link and send the traffic.

04

Measure it

The network fires your postback. The ledger matches the sale to the click id, subtracts spend, and shows net profit per day, campaign or lander variant.

The difference is the join

A checker does not know your profit. A tracker does not know your policy verdict. A page builder knows neither. Nobody keeps all three on the same ad.
