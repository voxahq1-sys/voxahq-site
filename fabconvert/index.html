What FabConvert is

FabConvert is a compliance, landing page and profit tracking tool for people who run paid ads. It checks ad copy against 89 policy rules before launch, rewrites the lines that would be rejected, builds the landing page the ad points to, and records what each click earned from your network postback. One account, one price, and every decision kept on the same record.

Questions, answered

Will this get my ad approved?

No, and any tool that says yes is guessing. It removes the written mistakes, which is where most rejections start. Platform review includes judgement nobody outside can see, and our policy page says so plainly.

Which platforms does it cover?

The rules are written against the published policies of the big ad platforms, so the copy check works for Meta, TikTok, Google and NewsBreak. The lander hosts on your own link, and the ledger takes a postback from any network.

Do I need an AI key or pay per check?

No. The rules run on our server, so there is no model call, no key to buy and no per check cost. Your plan is the only bill.

Is the rewrite done by AI?

No, it is a fixed rewrite map, which is why it is instant and free. It gets a draft back to publishable, not to perfect copy.

Can I keep my own tracker?

Yes. If you already run Voluum, RedTrack, ClickFlare or Everflow, keep it and use FabConvert for the check and the lander. The built in ledger is for people who do not want to pay for a tracker yet.

Can my team see the same rules?

Yes, on Agency. A buyer and a reviewer share one workspace, the same rules and the same approval log, and the log follows the account rather than the browser.

What happens when I hit the free limit?

The server stops the check and says how many you have used. It resets on the first of the month, and a refused check is not counted.

How do I cancel?

Reply to any email from us. The plan stays active until the end of the period you paid for, then drops to free. No form, no retention call.
