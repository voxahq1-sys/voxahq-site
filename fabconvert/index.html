How it works

Before launch: paste the ad into the checker, get red, amber or green, fix what is flagged, and the approval is recorded with a name and a timestamp.

After launch: the network fires your postback on every sale. The ledger matches it to the click id, adds your spend, and keeps a running net profit.

Later: when a platform rejects something that passed, that line becomes a rule. The checker gets sharper with every miss.

Questions

Do I need both features?

No. The checker works on its own, and so does the ledger. They are stronger together because one record then carries the verdict and the profit.

Which networks work with the postback?

Any network that lets you set a postback URL with a payout macro. ClickBank, Digistore24, BuyGoods, MaxWeb, and any tracker such as Voluum or RedTrack can send it.

Where is my data stored?

Conversions and spend entries are stored as key-value records on Cloudflare, keyed by click id. The compliance checker itself runs in your browser and stores nothing.

Can it be wrong?

The checker can pass something a reviewer later rejects, because platform review includes automated judgement. It does not guarantee approval. The ledger only reports what your postback and your spend entries say.
