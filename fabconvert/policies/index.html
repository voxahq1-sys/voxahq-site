Policies

Plain language, no legal padding. Last updated 22 November 2026.

What we store

  • Your email address, so you can sign in with a code. No password is stored anywhere.
  • Your workspace id, a short code derived from your email, used in your postback URL.
  • Postback records: click id, offer name, payout, status (sale or refund), transaction id and time.
  • Spend entries: an amount, an optional label and a timestamp.
  • Usage counts: how many checks you ran this month.
  • A hash of your API key, never the key itself.

We do not store the ad copy you check. The compliance check runs on the server, returns a verdict, and the text is not saved. The landing page is fetched once, only when you enter a URL.

What we do not do

  • No advertising trackers on FabConvert pages, no third-party analytics scripts.
  • No selling or sharing of your data with anyone.
  • No reading of your ad accounts. FabConvert never asks for ad platform credentials.

How long we keep it

Postback records are kept for 180 days, spend entries for 365 days, and usage counts for 70 days. After that they expire on their own. Your email stays as long as the account exists.

Deleting your data

Two buttons on the account page. Clear my ledger deletes every postback and spend entry. Delete my account data does that and signs you out. Both are immediate and cannot be undone.

Free plan and paid plans

The free plan allows 50 compliance checks a calendar month and unlimited postbacks. Paid plans raise the check allowance to 2,000 (Solo) or 10,000 (Agency). A check that is refused because you hit the limit is not counted.

Refunds and cancellation

Paid plans are billed monthly. If you cancel, the plan stays active until the end of the period you already paid for and then drops to free; we do not pro-rate partial months. If FabConvert did not work for you in the first 14 days of a paid plan, reply to any email from us within those 14 days and we refund that month in full, no questions and no form. After 14 days, refunds are at our discretion and we will say yes or no in one reply.

Nothing in this policy limits a refund right you have under the law where you live.

Service levels and limits

FabConvert is a small product. We aim for the checker and the postback endpoint to be available at all times, and we do not currently offer an uptime guarantee or a service credit. If the endpoint is down, your network can retry the postback later; duplicates are deduplicated by transaction id, so a retry is safe.

What FabConvert is not

It is not legal advice and it does not guarantee that a platform will approve an ad. Platform review includes automated judgement we cannot see. The checker catches the written rules, which is where most rejections start.
