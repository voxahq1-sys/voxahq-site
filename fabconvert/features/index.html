1. Compliance check

The module everything else hangs off.

What it does

Reads three inputs

  • The ad copy: primary text, headline, description
  • The text inside the creative, read from the image
  • The landing page, fetched once, only when you enter the URL
What you get

A verdict with reasons

  • Red, amber or green, plus a risk score
  • The exact line that is flagged and why
  • A safer rewrite, and a button that applies it and rechecks

89 rules across health claims, money promises, before and after, personal attributes, urgency, regulatory wording and format. 55 of them are hard blocks, 25 are warnings, 9 are style notes that cost nothing but weaken an ad.

Your own rules. When a platform rejects a line, you paste it into My rules. From then on that exact line is caught for your account. It is the only honest way a static checker gets better, and it costs nothing to run.

2. Lander builder

The page the ad points to, hosted on our domain, on your link.

Build

Four templates, three variants

  • Advertorial, quiz, listicle, comparison
  • Weight and country list per variant
  • Hero image upload, resized for you
  • Pixel and head script fields
Measure

Clicks and sales per variant

  • Views, clicks, CTR, sales and CVR
  • Last seven days, per page
  • A sale pulls traffic toward the winning variant
  • Public link uses a random id, never your workspace

3. Profit tracking

One postback URL. The maths is done for you.

In

What it takes

  • Your network fires the postback with the click id, payout and transaction id
  • Sale, refund, chargeback or lead
  • Spend entries you add by hand
Out

What it shows

  • Revenue, spend, net profit, ROI
  • Revenue per conversion
  • Duplicates deduped by transaction id, so a retry is safe
  • A refund comes out of revenue automatically

4. Approval log

The part an agency actually needs.

Every approval is stored on the account, not in the browser: the line, the verdict, the reviewer name and the time. Export to CSV to hand over with the work. It is what turns "I checked it" into a record.

What it does not do

It does not guarantee approval

Platform review includes judgement nobody outside can see. The checker removes written mistakes, which is where most rejections start.

It does not read before and after photos

It reads words, including words inside an image. Whether two photos are the same person, or the timeline is realistic, is a human call.

It does not touch your ad account

No platform credentials, no reading of your ad accounts, no launching. It never asks for access you would regret giving.
