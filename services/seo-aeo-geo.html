What AEO and GEO actually change

AEO (answer engine optimisation) is about the first 100 words: the page answers the question immediately, in plain language, with one number a person could quote.

GEO (generative engine optimisation) is about being readable by machines: schema, an llms.txt file, dated facts, and a clean robots.txt that lets GPTBot, PerplexityBot and ClaudeBot in.

Both are checkable. Every plan ends with a report showing what changed and which prompts now name you.
