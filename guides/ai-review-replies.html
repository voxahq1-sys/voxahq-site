AI review replies: what they cost and when to use them

Home/Guides/AI review replies

By the VOXA team · we check every price by hand before it goes up

Updated October 9, 20266 min readPrices checked Oct 2026

Quick answer

If you get a handful of reviews a month, reply yourself from your free Google Business Profile. Pay for a tool once volume or multiple locations make that a chore. Workable plans start around $15 a month.

  • Google Business Profile: reply to any review yourself, free.
  • Free AI generators: about five replies a day, no signup and no card.
  • A paid entry plan: around $15 a month for one location and up to 200 AI replies, with a human approval step.
  • An autopilot plan: around $29 a month for unlimited replies, auto-sync and auto-post.
  • The catch: an auto-reply to a one-star review can make things worse, so keep a human approval step.

Reviews are public work. Google rewards a profile that answers them, and a customer reads your reply before they read the review. The only question is whether to pay software to draft the replies for you.

When it is worth paying

Three signs say stop doing it by hand: you run more than one location, you get more than about 20 reviews a month, or replies are slipping and unanswered reviews are piling up. Below that, the free Google Business Profile is faster than setting up a tool.

The catch

The tool that drafts a reply is not the risky part. The risky part is publishing without reading. A cheerful auto-reply under a one-star review about a refund reads as tone-deaf and can turn a bad review into a screenshotted one. The tools worth using put a human approval step in front of publishing and check the draft against Google's policy. Run the first month on manual approval before you switch autopilot on, and keep a rule for anything one star or below.

One note: review software only pays off if you use it. Buy the cheap entry plan, prove you will actually review the drafts, and only then consider autopilot.

Frequently asked questions

How much do AI review reply tools cost?

Entry plans start around $15 a month for one location and up to 200 AI replies. Autopilot plans with unlimited replies cost around $29 a month. Free generators cover about five replies a day, and replying directly in your Google Business Profile is free.

Is it worth paying for review reply software?

Only past a certain volume. If you get more than about 20 reviews a month, run more than one location, or keep falling behind on replies, a $15 to $29 a month tool saves real time. Below that, reply yourself in the free Google Business Profile.

Should AI reply to Google reviews automatically?

Not without a human check. An auto-reply to a negative review can make a bad situation worse. Use the tool to draft, keep manual approval switched on, and review anything rated one or two stars by hand.

Does replying to reviews help local ranking?

Google says responding to reviews shows engagement, and an active profile with answered reviews signals that a business is operating. It is not a ranking lever on its own, but it affects the reviews a customer reads before choosing you.

What is the cheapest way to keep up with reviews?

Reply inside your free Google Business Profile, or use a free AI generator for the wording. Both cost nothing and cover low volume. Pay a tool only when volume or multiple locations make that a chore.
