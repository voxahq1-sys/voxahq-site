Buffer pricing: what $5 per channel actually costs a one-person business

Home/Guides/Buffer pricing

By the VOXA team · prices from Buffer’s own pricing page

Published October 19, 20265 min readPrices checked by hand

The short answer

Buffer moved to per-channel pricing. Essentials is $5 per channel a month on annual billing, Team is $10 per channel. Three channels is $15 a month, five channels is $25, and the limit is channels rather than posts, so posting more costs nothing extra.

What three channels costs

Instagram, Facebook and Pinterest is three channels. On Essentials at the annual rate that is $15 a month, or $60 per channel per year for one channel and $180 a year for three. Add LinkedIn and YouTube and you are at five channels, $25 a month.

The important number is not the monthly total. It is the marginal cost of starting a new platform, which is $5 a channel. That is the cheapest it gets to try a new channel for a month.

The catch nobody prints

Per-channel pricing is cheap for people who post a lot on few platforms and expensive for people who post a little on many. If you run six channels for a shop, one post a week each, you are paying $30 a month for six posts a week. At that volume a cheaper scheduler with a flat plan wins.

The other catch: the annual price is roughly 17% below monthly billing, but it is charged per channel, so adding platforms later means buying at the annual rate again or paying the monthly difference.

Scheduling is not the same as writing

A scheduler moves posts around. It does not write them. The hour saved on scheduling is smaller than the hour spent writing, and that is where AI actually helps: captions from a photo, three variants of a hook, a week of posts in one sitting.

If your bottleneck is moving data between tools rather than scheduling, the cheaper layer is a free automation tier. We priced that in what Zapier’s free plan includes, and the wider comparison is in Zapier vs Make vs n8n.

Questions people ask

Does Buffer charge per post?

No. On the plans we checked, the price is per connected channel. Posting more often on the same channel does not raise the bill.

Is annual billing worth it?

Only if you are sure about the channels. Annual billing is cheaper per month but locks each channel for a year.

What is the cheapest way to run three channels?

On Essentials annual pricing, three channels is about $15 a month. Compare that with the flat-rate schedulers before you commit, because the per-channel model punishes adding platforms.
