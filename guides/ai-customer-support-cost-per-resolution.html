How much does AI customer support cost per resolution?

Home/Guides/AI support cost per resolution

By the VOXA team · we check every price by hand before it goes up

Updated October 9, 2026, 20267 min readPrices checked Oct 2026

Quick answer

An AI-resolved support ticket costs $0.50 to $2.00. A human-handled one costs $6.00 to $13.50. Everything else is a detail about how each vendor packages that number.

  • Intercom Fin: $0.99 per resolution, charged only when the customer's issue is confirmed resolved.
  • Zendesk: about $1.50 per verified resolution on committed volume, or $2.00 pay-as-you-go, plus $55 to $115 per agent a month for the platform.
  • Gorgias: $0.60 to $1.27 per resolution, on top of tiered platform plans.
  • Freshdesk Freddy AI: $0.10 per session. Salesforce Agentforce: $2.00 per conversation or $0.10 per action.
  • Human agents cost $6.00 to $13.50 per contact, and the realistic net saving in year one is 20 to 35%.

Support is the easiest place to measure an AI agent, because you can count resolutions and divide by the bill. That is why every vendor now sells a number, usually a cost per resolution or per conversation.

What you actually save

The gap between $0.99 and $13.50 is real, but the net saving across a whole team is smaller than the gap suggests, because some tickets still need a person. Published benchmarks put the realistic first-year reduction at 20 to 35%. Ecommerce does best, around 47% average, because most of its tickets fall into predictable categories. Technical SaaS tickets cap near 55 to 65% AI resolution. Fintech resolves less but saves more per ticket, because a human handle time there is longer.

The catch: you only save on what resolves

An AI agent that answers a question and still leaves the customer needing a human has cost you twice. That is why the billable unit matters more than the headline price. Outcome-based pricing charges once, on success; per-conversation and per-session pricing charge whether or not the issue is fixed. Before you sign, ask two things: what counts as a resolution, and what happens to the tickets that do not resolve.

Frequently asked questions

How much does AI customer support cost per resolution?

Published rates run from about $0.10 per session (Freshdesk) to $2.00 per conversation (Agentforce). Intercom Fin charges $0.99 per resolved outcome, Zendesk about $1.50 per verified resolution, and Gorgias $0.60 to $1.27. The average AI interaction costs $0.50 to $2.00 all in.

How much does a human support ticket cost?

$6.00 to $13.50 per contact once you include salary, benefits, training, management overhead and idle time. Gartner's widely cited average is $13.50 per agent-assisted contact.

Is per-resolution pricing better than per-conversation?

For buyers, yes. Per-resolution charges only when the AI resolves the issue end-to-end, so you pay for value delivered. Per-conversation and per-session pricing charge even when the AI fails and the customer escalates, which means you pay twice.

What percentage of tickets can AI resolve?

Realistic first-year net savings across a whole team are 20 to 35%. Ecommerce reaches around 47% average cost reduction because most tickets are predictable. Technical SaaS tickets cap around 55 to 65% AI resolution.

Should a small business use an AI support agent?

Only once you have enough ticket volume to measure. Below a few hundred conversations a month, the platform fees and setup usually outweigh the per-resolution saving, and answering the questions yourself with better help pages is cheaper.
