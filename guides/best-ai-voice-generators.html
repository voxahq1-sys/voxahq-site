Best AI voice generators in 2026, priced per finished minute

Quick answer

For video voiceover, ElevenLabs Creator at $22 is the plan that gives you enough room to re-record. Murf charges by the character through its API, which suits automated pipelines. Speechify at $29 is the cheapest way to turn documents into audio, not the cheapest way to narrate a video.

  • Cheapest entry for narration: ElevenLabs Starter, $6/mo
  • Best per-character control: Murf, $0.01 per 1,000 characters on the API
  • Best for listening to your own documents: Speechify Premium, $29/mo

The only maths that matters: cost per finished minute

Voice tools meter differently, and that makes the headline price useless. ElevenLabs sells credits, roughly 1,000 per minute of speech. Murf sells characters, and 1,000 characters is about 15 to 18 seconds of speech at a normal pace. Speechify sells a subscription for listening.

So compare at the point your file is finished: a five-minute video needs about 5,000 ElevenLabs credits, or roughly 18,000 characters on Murf, which at $0.01 per 1,000 characters works out to about $0.18 of API usage. That gap is the whole story of this comparison.

ElevenLabs: pay for the plan, not the minutes

Starter at $6 covers 30,000 credits and, more importantly, the commercial licence. Creator at $22 raises that to 121,000 and adds professional voice cloning. Pro at $99 lifts output quality and gives you 600,000 credits.

The trap is editing. A five-minute script is rarely recorded once, and the second and third takes come out of the same credit bucket. Budget roughly double your finished runtime and the plan tier stops looking generous. If you only publish once a week, Creator is the sensible ceiling.

We keep the plan-by-plan breakdown on the ElevenLabs pricing page in Picks.

Murf: the per-character option

Murf's API pricing is the most transparent in this category: $0.01 per 1,000 characters for text to speech, $0.03 for speech to text, $0.10 per minute for translation. You are not buying a seat, you are buying output.

That suits two cases: a pipeline that generates many short clips, and a business that wants to forecast spend per project instead of guessing at a plan. It suits narration less well, because you lose the flat monthly ceiling that makes creative work cheap.

Speechify: a reading tool that also generates audio

Speechify's free tier gives you ten voices and playback up to 1.5x. Premium at $29 a month, or $139 billed yearly, unlocks 1,000+ voices, 60+ languages, 5x playback, voice typing and AI podcasts.

It is excellent at turning a PDF or a long article into something you can listen to. It is not the tool you reach for when you need a 40-second commercial read with the right pause before the product name.

What we did not test

We did not generate the same script on all three tools and compare the audio, and we have not measured licence terms case by case. Every price above comes from the vendor's pricing page on 2 November 2026. Before you pay, generate one paragraph on each free tier and listen; voice is the one category where the sample matters more than the spec sheet.
