AI agents for small business: what they do and what they cost

Home/Guides/AI agents for small business

By the VOXA team · we check every price by hand before it goes up

Updated October 7, 20266 min readPrices checked Oct 2026

Quick answer

Workflow agents inside an automation platform cost $9 to $30 a month. Customer support agents charge per resolved conversation, typically $0.40 to $0.99.

  • Make AI Agents sit inside plans from $9/mo; n8n agents are free self-hosted.
  • Intercom Fin costs from $0.99 per resolved outcome, plus $29/seat on the Essential plan.
  • Ticket triage add-ons run around $0.40 per resolved ticket.
  • In 400 tickets a month, a 60% resolution rate is about $238. Measure resolution rate and cost per resolution.

"AI agent" has become the most overused phrase in software. In practice, for a small business, it means one of three things: a support bot that answers customer questions, a workflow that makes decisions inside your existing automations, or a chat assistant that does a job your staff used to do by hand.

All three are buyable today. The pricing is the interesting part, because support agents usually charge per resolved conversation, not per seat.

Per resolution is the pricing model to understand

A support agent that costs $0.99 per resolved conversation sounds expensive next to a $29 seat. Do the maths on your own volume: 400 tickets a month at a 60% resolution rate is 240 resolutions, so about $238. Compare that with the hourly cost of the person who currently answers them, and the number usually looks fine. Then check what happens when the agent is wrong, because you still pay for the resolution and you still fix the problem.

Start with one narrow job

  • Triage, not answering. Let the agent categorise, tag and route incoming messages. Mistakes there are cheap.
  • Drafting, not sending. The agent writes the reply, a human approves it. Same efficiency gain, none of the brand risk.
  • Internal only. Point it at your own documentation so staff get answers without searching. Lowest risk, immediate time saving.
  • Data entry. Moving structured information between two systems is where agents are genuinely reliable today.

What agents still get wrong

They are confident when they should not be. They fail on multi-step tasks where a middle step changes the rules, and they rarely know when to stop and ask a person. Any process with money, refunds, legal language or a customer's account on the line should keep a human approval step, at least for the first few months.

Measure two numbers

Resolution rate: the share of conversations the agent closes without a human. Cost per resolution: total agent spend divided by those resolutions. If the first is climbing and the second is falling, keep going. If resolutions are flat but you are paying more, the agent is being used as an expensive search box.

What we would pay for

A small business with a support inbox: a helpdesk that includes an AI agent on the entry plan, and per-resolution pricing you can predict. Everyone else: start with workflow agents inside the automation platform you already pay for, because that is $9 to $30 a month rather than a new contract.

Frequently asked questions

What is an AI agent, in plain terms?

Software that takes a goal and completes several steps on its own, instead of waiting for you to click through each one. In a small business that usually means answering support messages, triaging tickets, or moving data between apps.

How much do AI agents cost for a small business?

Workflow agents are $9 to $30 a month inside an automation platform. Customer support agents charge from around $0.40 to $0.99 per resolved conversation, which for a few hundred tickets a month is often cheaper than a seat but less predictable.

Will an AI agent replace my support staff?

Not at the volumes most small businesses run. It absorbs repetitive questions and drafts replies, which frees the same people to handle the harder conversations. Plan for supervision, not replacement.

What should I automate first?

The task that runs most often and needs the least judgement: tagging and routing, meeting recaps, or copying data between two apps. Keep a human approval step on anything involving money or customer accounts.

Do I need technical skills to run one?

For hosted agents in a helpdesk or automation platform, no. Self-hosted options like n8n need someone comfortable with servers and API keys, which is the usual reason small businesses pick the hosted route instead.
