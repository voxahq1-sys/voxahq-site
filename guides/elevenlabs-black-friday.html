The short answer

ElevenLabs runs its Black Friday deal from 16 November to 1 December 2026: 55% off annual plans, which puts the Creator plan at $99 for the year instead of about $220. Monthly subscribers on Pro can switch to annual at 50% off, and Business annual plans get three months free. If you were going to buy a year anyway, this is the cheapest window of 2026. If you were going to stay monthly, the discount is not a reason to lock in.

What the deal actually is

Three separate offers, all inside the same window (16 November to 1 December 2026):

  • 55% off annual plans. Applies across the annual line, and takes Creator to $99 for a full year.
  • 50% off for Pro subscribers who switch from monthly to annual. This one is only for existing monthly Pro accounts, which matters because Pro is where API users live.
  • Three months free on Business annual. Worth roughly a quarter of the annual price if you are running a team workspace.

The discount is on the annual commitment, not on a trial or a first month. That is the whole shape of it: cheaper per month, but you pay for twelve months up front.

Is $99 for Creator actually a good deal

Creator is the plan that includes professional voice cloning and 121,000 credits a month. At the normal $22 a month you pay $264 across a year. At $99 you pay less than five months of the monthly price for twelve months of access.

That is a real saving, but only under one condition: you were going to use it for most of the year. If your project ends in February, paying $99 up front instead of $22 for two months is a $55 loss. The discount rewards commitment, not curiosity.

A second condition matters more for anyone publishing: the commercial licence is what you are buying, not the credits. Starter at $6 gives you the licence with 30,000 credits; Creator gives you the licence plus cloning plus four times the credits. If you only needed the licence, the discount does not change your answer.

What to do if you are already paying monthly

Check which plan you are on before 16 November, because the switching discount is not the same for every tier.

  • On Creator monthly: the $99 annual offer is the one to take. No switch discount needed; the annual price is already the deal.
  • On Pro monthly: the 50% annual switch is worth more than a straight annual purchase, so use the switch path rather than cancelling and resubscribing.
  • On Business: three months free is the equivalent of the discount, and it applies to the annual plan only.
  • On free or Starter: nothing to switch. Decide on usage, not on the discount.

The contest nobody asked about

ElevenLabs is running a #MoreThanVoice contest alongside the sale: $10,000 daily winners from 16 to 30 November and a $50,000 grand prize. Entry is free, open to free and paid users, and takes two steps: create something in ElevenCreative, then post it on two platforms with the hashtag and the watermark. It is a marketing campaign, but the prizes are real and the entry cost is a video you were making anyway.

Dates worth putting in a calendar

  • 1 December 2026: sale and contest close.

The 16th is the day to buy if you have already decided, because ElevenLabs prices annual plans in dollars and the deal is not guaranteed to run to the last day.
