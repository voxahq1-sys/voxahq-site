About

Who runs this site

I am Raghav Walia, 20, based in Panchkula, India. I test the free tiers of AI tools, write down what each one actually costs after the free plan ends, and publish the date I checked it. Everything on this site comes from accounts I use myself, not from press releases.

When a tool is not worth the money I say so on its page. That is why the site has no affiliate-driven ordering: the price table is the product.

Alongside the directory I take on a small number of SEO clients and run a free 48-hour audit, because the same checks I do here are what most small sites are missing.
