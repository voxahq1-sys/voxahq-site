03 — Why the free plan is not the product

The limit is the feature

Free tiers are not smaller versions of the paid plan. They are the paid plan with one working part removed, and which part is removed tells you who the plan is really for.

Chat limits

Message caps reset on a window. Starting a new chat does not reset the clock. Long documents hit the cap before a long conversation does.

Tool limits

Files, images and research runs are metered separately from chat, so a generous chat plan can still refuse your fourth upload.

Output rights

Some plans watermark output or restrict commercial use on the free tier. That is a cost even when the price says zero.
