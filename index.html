Questions people ask

Short answers

Is VOXA free to read?

Yes. No paywall, no signup, no email required. There are no affiliate links on this site, and nothing here earns us a commission.

How do you check the prices in your guides?

We read the numbers off each vendor's own pricing page, note the month we checked them, and link you straight to those pages so you can confirm them yourself. If a price has changed, tell us and we update the guide.

Do you accept paid placements or sponsored rankings?

No. No brand can buy a verdict, a score, or a place on a list. If we ever run advertising, the ads are labelled where they appear.

How often are the guides updated?

Every guide carries a date. AI pricing and features move every few weeks, so we revisit the guides people read most and re-date them when the numbers change.

Who writes VOXA?

A small team writes and edits the site. We use AI tools to help draft and check, but every price and claim is verified by hand before it goes up.

Can I suggest a tool or ask a question?

Please do. Use the contact page, or email voxa.hq1@gmail.com. Reader questions are how most of our guides start.
