What AI voice costs per finished minute

Home/Notes/Voice cost per minute

By the VOXA team · we check every price by hand before it goes up

November 2, 20263 min readPrices checked Nov 2026

Three voice tools, three different meters. The sticker price tells you nothing until you convert it to a finished minute.

The conversion, in one table

ToolHow it metersCost of one finished minute
ElevenLabs CreatorCredits, ~1,000 per minute$22 covers ~121 minutes a month
Murf APICharacters, $0.01 per 1,000Roughly $0.04 per minute
Speechify PremiumFlat subscription$29 a month, unlimited listening

Prices checked on each vendor's page, 2 November 2026. Character-to-minute conversion assumes about 1,000 characters per 15 seconds at normal pace.

What the gap means

If you are generating ten minutes a month, ElevenLabs Creator costs $22 and Murf's API costs about $0.40. The gap is not a discount, it is a different product: one sells a studio with cloning and quality tiers, the other sells characters.

So the question is not which is cheaper. It is whether you need the studio. For narration you will re-record, buy the subscription and stop counting. For generated clips at volume, count characters and pay per output.
