n8n Community Edition vs n8n Cloud: the break-even

Home/Notes/n8n Community vs Cloud

By the VOXA team · prices from n8n's own pricing page, checked October 2026

Published October 16, 20264 min readAnchors verified

The short answer

The Community Edition has no licence fee at all, so self-hosting costs the server: about 4 to 12 euros a month. Cloud starts at 24 euros and reaches 800 euros a month at the top tier. At low volume the VPS wins on price; the cloud wins on not owning a server.

How to work out your own break-even

  1. Count executions. A workflow that runs hourly is about 720 a month. Twenty of those is 14,400.
  2. Find the cloud plan that covers that number and note its price.
  3. Add your maintenance time at your own hourly rate, two to four hours a month, to the 4 to 12 euro VPS.
  4. Compare the two totals, not the two headline prices.

At the low end, a few hundred executions a month, the comparison is roughly 6 euros against 24. At that point the licence difference is trivial and the decision is really about who maintains the thing. At high volume the gap widens to hundreds of euros a month, and the maintenance hours start to look cheap.

What the cloud buys you

  • Managed uptime, so a failed workflow at the weekend is not your weekend
  • No server patching, no SSL renewal, no backup script to write
  • A support channel, which matters the week something breaks badly

What it does not buy is more features. The Community Edition runs the same engine, with no execution cap, and the execution history limit is the main practical difference at high volume.

When to switch in either direction

Go self-hosted when volume is growing, when data should stay on your own machine, or when you already run a server. Go cloud when the person maintaining the server leaves, when you need an uptime commitment, or when the monthly difference is smaller than an hour of your time.

Questions people ask

Is the Community Edition free?

Yes for the software, with no execution cap. The cost is your server, about 4 to 12 euros a month, plus your own time.

When is the cloud worth it?

When the plan price is smaller than the value of the hours you would spend maintaining a server, or when nobody wants to own that job.

How do I compare tiers?

Count monthly executions first (hourly equals 720 each), find the tier that covers it, then compare its price against a VPS plus your time.
