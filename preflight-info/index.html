Catch the rejection before you spend.

Preflight checks ad copy, the text inside the creative, and the landing page against 46 health, finance and platform rules. It runs before launch, so a rejection costs you nothing.

Most rejections are not mysterious. They are the same fifteen claims in different words: a cure, a guaranteed result, a number of pounds in a number of weeks, a question about the reader's body, a before and after, a percentage nobody can substantiate. Preflight names the line, the rule behind it, and the rewrite that keeps the offer.

What it does

Bulk check

Paste up to 200 ads. Each gets red, amber or green, with the top issue named. Export the results as CSV.

Claims library

Add the phrases your legal team already cleared. They pass instead of blocking, so you stop re-arguing the same sentence.

Approval log

Approve or reject with your name on it. Every decision is timestamped and exportable for the client file.

Image text

Upload the creative and the tool reads the words inside it, because half the claims hide in the picture.

Landing page

Fetches the page, checks the tracking parameters, flags the risky claims there, and warns when the page does not match the ad.

API

One POST returns a verdict per ad, so a launcher can gate every push without a human in the loop.

How accurate is it

Two tests, both published here rather than claimed:

  • 25 ads with documented rejections and their stated reasons: 25 flagged, 24 of them hard blocks.
  • 52 live ads running on Meta today: zero false alarms after the rules were tightened.
It does not guarantee approval. Platform review is partly automated judgement. Preflight catches the written rules, which is where most rejections start, and the log shows exactly what was cleared and by whom.
