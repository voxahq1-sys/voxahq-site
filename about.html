What each AI tool costs, dated

VOXA is run by one person. Every price on this site is taken from the vendor official pricing page, with the date it was checked.

Raghav Walia

I am 20, based in Panchkula, India, and I write and edit everything you read on VOXA. I started this site because most AI tool reviews quote the headline price and skip the part that matters: what happens when the free plan runs out, and which limit you hit first.

The method is simple. The free plan is described from the vendor own help pages, the paid price is taken from their pricing page, and the date it was checked is printed on every page. If a tool is not worth the money, the page says that too, even when a cheaper alternative would earn me a commission elsewhere.

No press releases are published as reviews. If a page does not have a check date on it, it is not finished.
