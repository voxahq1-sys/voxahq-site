About VOXA

Home/About Us

Another AI tool launches every week with a landing page that claims to save you ten hours. VOXA is where those claims get put to work first.

We take a task people actually do for a living, work out which tools fit it, and then check every price, plan limit and feature claim against the vendor's own pages. The result is a short guide: what the tool does, what it costs once the free tier runs out, where it stumbles, and who should skip it.

What VOXA covers

Writing and research, automation between apps, assistants and agents, image and video generation, voice and meeting notes, and the spreadsheet work nobody enjoys. Software only. We are not a news site and we do not chase funding announcements.

How a guide gets made

  • The task comes first. Most topics arrive as a reader question: two tools, one job, no clear answer.
  • The claims get checked. Prices, plan limits and features come from the vendor's own pricing and documentation pages, not from a marketing summary.
  • The dull parts get written. Price, limits, exports, data terms, and the point at which the tool stops being useful.
  • The page stays alive. Each guide carries a date. When pricing or features move, we rewrite rather than leave a stale verdict online.

What we refuse to do

No ranking for sale. No invented numbers. No paid placements dressed up as recommendations, and no pretending we spent a week inside a tool when we did not. If a brand asks for a positive verdict in exchange for money or traffic, the answer is no.

Who pays for this site

VOXA is free to read. There are no affiliate links on this site, and nothing here earns us a commission. When we run advertising, the ads are labelled where they appear. No brand can buy a verdict, a score, or a place on a list.

Found something wrong? Send the page and the detail. Corrections are published quickly, and the guide gets re-dated so readers can see it moved.
