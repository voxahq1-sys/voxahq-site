Hi, I’m Raghav Walia.

I’m a student, and I’ve been using AI tools for the last 8-9 months. Mostly ChatGPT, Claude and Canva, and I’m still learning new tools as I go.

Why I built VOXA

When I decided to upgrade to a paid AI plan, I was surprised by how much the final bill was. The pricing wasn’t clear on any one platform, and I couldn’t find a simple page that told me the real cost before I paid. Most websites only say “free to start” and leave out the limits, the upgrade you get pushed into, and what you pay once everything is added up.

That’s why I started VOXA. It’s the guide I wished I had before I paid for my first AI subscription.

What you’ll find here

Every guide on VOXA covers four things:

  • The price: what each plan costs, and what the free tier really includes
  • The catch: the limits, locked features and things that quietly don’t work
  • The setup: steps you can copy yourself
  • The verdict: buy, wait, or skip

Every price carries a “last checked” date. If something has changed, tell me and I’ll fix it.

How VOXA makes money

VOXA is free to read, with no signup and no paywall. The site may earn money through affiliate links and Google AdSense ads. If you buy through an affiliate link, I may earn a small commission at no extra cost to you. This never changes what I recommend or the verdict I give. You can read more on the Affiliate Disclosure and Editorial Policy pages.
