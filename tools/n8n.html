n8n pricing and free plan limits

Paid plans from €20. Prices checked 17 November 2026 against the vendor's pricing page.

n8n is free to self-host with no execution cap, and its Cloud plans start at 20 euros a month billed yearly for 2,500 executions. Pro is 50 euros for 10,000 and Business is $800.

What the free plan gives you

  • Community edition, self-hosted, free and unmetered
  • Unlimited users and workflows, every integration
  • No SSO, Git, environments or shared projects
  • You pay for the server and your own maintenance
The limit that breaks first: self-hosting. The licence is free, the server is not, and neither is the evening you spend upgrading it.

What you actually need

  • Technical and happy to run a box: Community is free and the best value in automation.
  • Want it managed: Cloud Starter at €20 covers 2,500 executions.
  • Production workflows: Pro at €50 for 10,000 executions and 20 concurrent runs.
  • Team with compliance needs: Business, which is where SSO and Git arrive.

Cheaper alternatives

Zapier starts at $19.99 for 750 tasks but bills per step, so a 10-step Zap burns 10 tasks. Make is cheaper per operation than Zapier but still hosted. n8n wins on complex workflows and loses on setup time.

Questions people ask

Is n8n free?

The Community edition is free and self-hosted, with no execution cap. You pay for the server it runs on.

How much is n8n Cloud?

Starter is €20 a month billed annually (€24 monthly) for 2,500 executions, and Pro is €50 (€60 monthly) for 10,000. Checked 17 November 2026.

What counts as an execution?

One run of a whole workflow, however many steps it has. That is the main difference from Zapier.

Can I self-host the paid features?

SSO, Git and environments require Business or Enterprise.

Is n8n good for a solo builder?

Yes. Self-hosted it is the cheapest serious automation stack, once you can run a server.

Does n8n charge per user?

No. Users and workflows are unlimited on every plan.

Verdict

Start on the cheapest tier that covers the limit you actually hit. For most people that is one step up from free, not the top plan, and yearly billing is usually 15 to 20 percent lower.
