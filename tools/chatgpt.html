ChatGPT pricing and free plan limits

$0 on Free, $8 on Go, $20 on Plus, $100-$500 on Pro. Prices read from the vendor's own page.

ChatGPT costs $0 on Free, $8 on Go, $20 on Plus and $100 to $500 on Pro. The free plan gives unlimited text chats but about three file uploads a day and a 27K token context.

This page prints the paid prices, the free-tier limits and the point each plan breaks. It is the same structure as every other entry in the index, so you can compare without reading a review.

What the free plan actually gives you

  • Unlimited text chats on GPT-6 Luna, subject to abuse guardrails.
  • About 3 file uploads a day. OpenAI's own FAQ sets this; the pricing page only says "limited messages with uploads".
  • Roughly 2 to 3 images a day. OpenAI does not publish the number; this is the observed ceiling.
  • 27K token context, about twelve pages of text you can paste into one conversation.
  • Limited voice, deep research, memory and Codex, and ads may appear on Free and Go.
The limit that breaks first: if you work with documents, it is the upload cap. Three files a day finishes before lunch, and no amount of new chats resets it.

Prices in USD. OpenAI can change them, so confirm on its pricing page before paying. Annual billing exists only on Business.

The difference between Plus and Pro is usage, not features

Pro does not unlock a secret better model. It raises the ceilings: more messages, 128K context instead of 54K, unlimited image creation, longer Codex sessions and $100 to $500 a month of API credit depending on the tier. If you are asking whether you need Pro, you almost certainly do not. The jump from Free to Plus at $20 is the one that changes what you can finish.

What you actually need

  • Light daily use, mostly chat: Free is enough. Add nothing.
  • Working with documents or images every day: Go at $8 if you want the cheapest step up, Plus at $20 if you also want the better model and no ads.
  • Work use, code, research, projects: Plus. This is the plan most people should be on.
  • All-day heavy use: Pro 100 at $100. Only go higher when the limit is stopping real work, not when the plan sounds impressive.

Cheaper alternatives worth comparing

Claude is $20 a month on Pro and is the stronger writer in our five-prompt test. Gemini is $19.99 and bundles with a Google account. Perplexity is $20 and is built for search rather than drafting. If your work is mostly reading and summarising sources, Perplexity's free tier may cover it before any of these paid plans does.

Questions people ask

Is ChatGPT free?

Yes. The Free plan costs $0 and gives unlimited everyday text chats on GPT-6 Luna. Uploads, image creation, voice, deep research, memory and Codex are limited separately, and ads may appear on Free.

How much does ChatGPT cost?

Go is $8 a month, Plus is $20 a month, and Pro comes in three tiers at $100, $200 and $500 a month. Business is $25 per seat per month, or $20 billed annually.

What is the catch with the free plan?

The chat is unlimited, the tools are not. Free gets about three file uploads a day, a couple of images a day, and a 27K token context, roughly twelve pages of text in one conversation.

Is Plus worth $20?

If you use it for work, yes. Plus unlocks GPT-6 Sol, expanded uploads and messages, no ads, deep research, projects, scheduled tasks and Codex. If you only chat, Go at $8 covers it.

Do the paid plans show ads?

Plus and above are ad-free. Free and Go may show ads.

Can I use ChatGPT for commercial work?

Yes. Output can be used commercially on paid plans. On Free, check OpenAI terms for your use case before publishing.

What is the difference between Plus and Pro?

Pro is about usage, not features: more messages, more context (128K tokens), unlimited image creation and monthly API credits. Plus is the right plan for most people.

Is there a student discount?

OpenAI does not publish a general student discount. ChatGPT for Teachers is free for verified US K-12 educators through June 2027.

Verdict

Start on Free and watch which limit you hit. If it is the upload cap or image creation, one step up fixes it, and Go at $8 is the cheapest fix. If it is the model, Plus at $20 is the only answer, and it is worth it for anyone using ChatGPT as a work tool rather than a novelty.

Free plan limits

The short answer: the ChatGPT free plan gives unlimited text chats but roughly three file uploads a day, a 27K token context, and no access to the newest models once capacity is tight. Go is $8 a month, Plus is $20, and Pro runs $100 to $500.

What the free plan gives you

  • Unlimited text chats on the free models
  • About three file or image uploads a day
  • A 27K token context window
  • Limited access to the newest models when capacity allows
  • Web search, voice mode and memory (capped)

The limits that actually stop you

LimitWhat it means
MessagesUnlimited, but the model switches to a lighter version at peak times
File uploadsAbout 3 a day on the free plan
Context27K tokens, which is roughly 20,000 words
Newest modelsLimited window, not guaranteed

Where it breaks in real use

The free plan breaks on two things: documents and deadlines. Upload a 40-page PDF and you run out of the three-a-day allowance before lunch. Come back at 9pm on a weekday and the heavy model may not be there.

How to make the free plan last longer

  • Batch your document work. With about three uploads a day, do every PDF in one session instead of spreading them across the week.
  • Paste text instead of uploading files. A long paste uses the context window without spending one of the upload slots.
  • Use the lighter model for drafts and save the strong model for the final pass, since peak-hour access is the scarce part.
  • Keep one thread per project. Memory and context carry better in a long conversation than in ten short ones.

What the paid plans cost

Go is $8 a month, Plus is $20, Pro is $100 to $500. Go exists for people who only need more messages; Plus is the first plan with priority access to the strong models.

Who the free plan is actually for

  • Testing whether an assistant fits your workflow at all
  • Personal use where a handful of questions a day is normal
  • Drafting and rewriting short text that does not need the newest model

Cheaper ways to get the same work done

Claude free is a fair comparison with a stricter rolling window, Gemini free runs a smaller model, and Perplexity free is better for sourced answers than long documents.

Verdict: ChatGPT free is worth using until you hit the limit named above, and not one task longer. If the work is paid work, budget for the $20-class plan instead of rationing a free tier that was never built for production.

Questions people ask

Is the free plan enough for a small business?

Usually for one person testing the tool, rarely once the output goes to a client. The limit that decides it is not the message count, it is the licence, the seat count or the file size.

Do the limits change without notice?

Yes. Vendors adjust free tiers quietly, usually by tightening a cap rather than removing a feature. That is why our pricing pages carry the date each figure was read.

Should I upgrade or switch tools?

Upgrade if the tool is where your work already lives and the limit is volume. Switch if the limit is a licence or a seat count, because paying more for the same restriction is the worst of both.
