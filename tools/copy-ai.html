Copy.ai pricing and free plan limits

Paid plans from $24. Prices checked 17 November 2026 against the vendor's pricing page.

Copy.ai has no free plan. The entry Chat tier costs $24 a month billed yearly, or $29 monthly, and bundles five seats, so a solo user pays for seats they will not use.

What the free plan gives you

  • No free plan
  • Entry tier bundles five seats
  • Cheapest paid tier is $24/mo yearly, $29 monthly
  • Growth tier above it for workflow volume
The limit that breaks first: there is no free tier and no single-seat plan, so a solo user pays for five seats to get one.

What you actually need

  • Solo user: a $20 chat plan is cheaper and better.
  • Small team running repeatable workflows: Copy.ai makes sense at $24 for five seats.
  • Just copywriting: Claude or ChatGPT at $20.

Cheaper alternatives

ChatGPT and Claude at $20 each beat it for solo writing. Jasper is the closer comparison and is more expensive. If workflow automation is the real need, n8n self-hosted is free.

Questions people ask

Is Copy.ai free?

No. The entry plan is paid.

How much does Copy.ai cost?

$24 a month billed yearly, or $29 monthly, for five seats on the Chat tier. Checked 17 November 2026.

Why five seats?

The entry tier is packaged for small teams, so one person pays for all five.

Is it good for workflows?

Yes, that is the current product focus, but workflow volume is capped per tier.

Is it cheaper than Jasper?

Yes, $24 against $59 per user.

Can I cancel?

Yes, at the end of the billing period.

Verdict

Start on the cheapest tier that covers the limit you actually hit. For most people that is one step up from free, not the top plan, and yearly billing is usually 15 to 20 percent lower.
