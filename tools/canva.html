Canva pricing and free plan limits

Paid plans from $18. Prices checked 17 November 2026 against the vendor's pricing page.

Canva is free with up to 20 AI uses a month and 5GB of storage. Pro costs $18 a month or $144 a year, and Business is $25 a month per person with shared brand controls.

What the free plan gives you

  • 1.6M+ templates, 4.7M+ assets, 5GB storage
  • Up to 20 Standard or Premium AI uses a month
  • 1 Brand Kit, 3 colours only
  • No background remover, no scheduling
The limit that breaks first: the AI counter. Pro does not mean unlimited AI: it means ten times the free allowance, and heavy image generation can still run out.

What you actually need

  • Occasional posts: Free covers it.
  • Social media every week: Pro at $18 for scheduling and background removal.
  • Team sharing brand assets: Business per seat.
  • Heavy AI generation: Canva is not the cheapest tool for that; use a dedicated image model.

Cheaper alternatives

Adobe Express sits at a similar price with stronger photo editing. Figma is better for product design. If you only need AI images, Midjourney or Ideogram cost less than a Canva Pro seat.

Questions people ask

Is Canva free?

Yes, with 20 AI uses a month, 5GB of storage and one brand kit limited to three colours.

How much is Canva Pro?

$18 a month, or $144 a year. Checked 17 November 2026.

Does Pro give unlimited AI?

No. It gives ten times the free allowance, plus an optional AI Pass add-on.

Can two people use one Pro account?

No. Pro is one seat; teams need Business.

What is missing from Free?

Background remover, premium templates, social scheduling and most AI tools.

Is there a free trial?

Yes, a 30-day trial of Pro, and the AI allowance during the trial matches Free.

Verdict

Start on the cheapest tier that covers the limit you actually hit. For most people that is one step up from free, not the top plan, and yearly billing is usually 15 to 20 percent lower.
