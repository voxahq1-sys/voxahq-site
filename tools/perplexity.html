Perplexity pricing and free plan limits

Paid plans from $20. Prices checked 17 November 2026 against the vendor's pricing page.

Perplexity is an answer engine: it searches the web and writes an answer with numbered sources. The free plan is genuinely usable for quick questions, but it caps the good search at three Pro Searches a day and one deep research query a month.

What the free plan gives you

  • Practically unlimited basic searches with citations
  • 3 Pro Searches per day
  • 1 Deep Research query per month
  • No model choice, no image generation, limited file uploads
The limit that breaks first: the Pro Search cap. Basic searches stay unlimited, which hides the fact that the good model is metered at three a day.

What you actually need

  • Quick facts with sources: Free covers it.
  • Daily research: Pro at $20, or $17 on annual billing.
  • Student: Education Pro at $10 with verification.
  • Building reports daily: Max, but only after Pro runs out.

Cheaper alternatives

ChatGPT at $20 and Gemini at $19.99 both do research, but neither cites sources as consistently. Claude at $20 is the better writer once the research is done.

Questions people ask

Is Perplexity free?

Yes. Basic searches are practically unlimited with citations, plus 3 Pro Searches a day and one research query a month.

How much is Perplexity Pro?

$20 a month, or $17 a month billed annually. Checked 17 November 2026.

What counts as a Pro Search?

A search that uses the advanced model with deeper retrieval. Basic searches do not count.

Is there a student plan?

Yes, Education Pro at $10 a month for verified students.

Does Perplexity show ads?

Paid plans are ad-free. The free tier may show sponsored follow-up questions.

Can I use it commercially?

Yes, subject to Perplexity's terms.

Verdict

Start on the cheapest tier that covers the limit you actually hit. For most people that is one step up from free, not the top plan, and yearly billing is usually 15 to 20 percent lower.
