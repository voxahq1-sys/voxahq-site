Claude pricing and free plan limits

Paid plans from $20. Prices checked 17 November 2026 against the vendor's pricing page.

Claude is Anthropic's chat assistant, and it is the one most writers and analysts prefer. The free plan is real but tightly metered: usage limits reset every five hours and again over a week, so a heavy afternoon can leave you locked out until the window rolls.

What the free plan gives you

  • Limited usage every 5 hours and every week
  • Web, desktop and mobile chat
  • Web search, file creation, running code
  • Memory across conversations, Artifacts, Claude Design
The limit that breaks first: the five-hour window, not the daily one. Long documents and repeated re-writes burn it fast, and starting a new chat does not reset the clock.

What you actually need

  • Occasional writing help: Free is enough, plan around the reset.
  • Writing every day: Pro at $20. The annual price is $17/mo if you pay $200 up front.
  • Code and long documents: Pro, because Claude Code and Projects are on it.
  • Agency work: Team seats, priced per person.

Cheaper alternatives

ChatGPT at $20 gives you unlimited text chats on a cheaper tier. Gemini at $19.99 bundles with Google apps. If your work is research-heavy, Perplexity at $20 is built for citations.

Questions people ask

Is Claude free?

Yes. The Free plan costs $0 with limited usage every 5 hours and every week, and includes web search, file creation, code running, memory and Artifacts.

How much is Claude Pro?

$20 a month, or $17 a month if you pay $200 for a year up front. Prices checked 17 November 2026.

What is the free-plan limit?

A rolling five-hour window plus a weekly cap. Starting a new chat does not reset either.

Is Claude better than ChatGPT?

In our five-prompt test Claude wrote the better essay and the better code, but it broke a word limit. ChatGPT won on following instructions.

Do I need Max?

Only if Pro stops you mid-work several times a day. Most people never hit it.

Does Claude have a student discount?

No general student discount is published.

Verdict

Start on the cheapest tier that covers the limit you actually hit. For most people that is one step up from free, not the top plan, and yearly billing is usually 15 to 20 percent lower.
