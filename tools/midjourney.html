Midjourney pricing and free plan limits

Paid plans from $10. Prices checked 17 November 2026 against the vendor's pricing page.

Midjourney has no free plan. Basic costs $10 a month or $8 if billed yearly for about 200 image jobs, Standard is $30, Pro $60 and Mega $120, all billed per month.

What the free plan gives you

  • No free tier and no free trial
  • Basic plan starts at $10 a month
  • One image job produces four images
  • General commercial terms included on paid plans
The limit that breaks first: there is no trial. The first payment is your test, so buy one month of Basic and decide before renewing.

What you actually need

  • Testing quality: one month of Basic at $10.
  • Regular creative work: Standard at $30 for unlimited relaxed generations.
  • Client work needing stealth: Pro at $60.
  • High-volume studio: Mega.

Cheaper alternatives

Ideogram and Flux have free tiers, so you can compare before paying Midjourney anything. Canva Pro at $18 includes AI image tools if you mostly need designs rather than art.

Questions people ask

Is Midjourney free?

No. There is no free plan and no trial.

How much is Midjourney?

Basic is $10 a month ($8 on annual), Standard $30, Pro $60 and Mega $120. Checked 17 November 2026.

What is an image job?

One prompt that returns four images. Basic gives about 200 jobs a month.

Can I use images commercially?

Yes, on paid plans under the general commercial terms.

Why is Standard so much more?

Unlimited relaxed generations and 15 hours of fast GPU time.

Is there a yearly discount?

Yes, 20 percent off when billed annually.

Verdict

Start on the cheapest tier that covers the limit you actually hit. For most people that is one step up from free, not the top plan, and yearly billing is usually 15 to 20 percent lower.
