Notion pricing and free plan limits

Paid plans from $20. Prices checked 17 November 2026 against the vendor's pricing page.

Notion is free for personal use, Plus costs $10 a member a month billed yearly, and Business costs $20 and is the cheapest plan that actually includes Notion AI rather than a trial.

What the free plan gives you

  • Free workspace for personal use
  • Limited Notion AI trial
  • 5MB file uploads
  • 30-day page history
The limit that breaks first: AI is not included on the cheap plans. Plus looks like the obvious step up and still leaves you on a trial.

What you actually need

  • Personal notes: Free.
  • Small team, no AI: Plus at $10.
  • You actually want AI: Business at $20 per member, which is where the agent lives.
  • Compliance: Enterprise.

Cheaper alternatives

Coda bundles AI differently and Obsidian has no subscription at all. If AI is the main reason you are looking at Notion, a $20 ChatGPT or Claude plan does more for less than a Business seat.

Questions people ask

Is Notion AI free?

Only as a limited trial on Free and Plus. The full agent requires Business.

How much is Notion?

Plus is $10 a member a month billed yearly, Business is $20. Notion AI is on Business. Checked 17 November 2026.

What are AI credits?

Business includes 1,500 Standard and 500 Select credits per member a month.

Can I buy AI separately?

No. It comes with Business or Enterprise.

Does the free plan have a page limit?

No page limit, but 5MB uploads and 30-day history.

Is there a yearly discount?

Yes, yearly billing is cheaper than monthly on Plus and Business.

Verdict

Start on the cheapest tier that covers the limit you actually hit. For most people that is one step up from free, not the top plan, and yearly billing is usually 15 to 20 percent lower.
