Gemini pricing and free plan limits

Paid plans from $19.99. Prices checked 17 November 2026 against the vendor's pricing page.

Gemini is Google's assistant and the cheapest serious paid tier in the market: Google AI Plus is $4.99 a month. The free plan runs on the Flash-Lite model with 32K context, so it is fine for questions and poor for long documents.

What the free plan gives you

  • Free with a Google account, on the Flash-Lite model
  • 32K context
  • Image creation, Deep Research, Gemini Live, Canvas, Gems
  • 15GB of Google storage
The limit that breaks first: the model. Free is the small model, and the good model is the paid tier, so the free plan is not the same product at a slower speed.

Gemini plans and prices

What you actually need

  • Already in Google apps: AI Plus at $4.99 is the cheapest way to get more usage.
  • Long documents or 1M context: AI Pro at $19.99.
  • Heavy daily use: Ultra, only if the Pro limits stop you.
  • Just asking questions: Free is enough.

Cheaper alternatives

ChatGPT at $20 has unlimited free text chats on its cheaper tier. Claude at $20 writes better. Perplexity at $20 is better for sourced research.

Questions people ask

Is Gemini free?

Yes, with a Google account, on the Flash-Lite model with 32K context and standard limits.

How much does Gemini cost?

Google AI Plus is $4.99, AI Pro is $19.99, and AI Ultra is $99.99 or $199.99 a month. Checked 17 November 2026.

What is the difference between Free and AI Pro?

Four times the usage, the better model, 1M context and 5TB of storage.

Is Gemini in Gmail included?

From Google AI Pro upwards.

Can I use Gemini for commercial work?

Yes, subject to Google's terms for the plan you are on.

Is there a yearly discount?

Yes, Google prices annual billing lower than monthly on all three paid tiers.

Verdict

Start on the cheapest tier that covers the limit you actually hit. For most people that is one step up from free, not the top plan, and yearly billing is usually 15 to 20 percent lower.
