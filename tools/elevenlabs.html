ElevenLabs pricing and free plan limits

Paid plans from $6. Prices checked 17 November 2026 against the vendor's pricing page.

ElevenLabs is free for 10,000 credits a month, about ten minutes of speech, with no commercial licence. Starter is $6 a month, Creator $22, Pro $99, Scale $299 and Business $990.

What the free plan gives you

  • 10,000 credits per month, about 10 minutes of speech
  • Text to speech, speech to text, sound effects, voice design
  • No commercial licence
  • 3 projects in Studio
The limit that breaks first: the commercial licence. Free output cannot be used commercially, so the first paid tier exists for the licence as much as the credits.

What you actually need

  • Testing voices: Free. Ten minutes is enough to judge quality.
  • Publishing anything: Starter at $6, because of the licence.
  • Weekly narration: Creator at $22 for the credit headroom.
  • Client work at volume: Pro or Scale.

Cheaper alternatives

Azure Speech is cheaper per character but sounds flatter. Play.ht sits at a similar price. If you narrate long videos, local tools like Piper cost nothing per character and need no subscription at all.

Questions people ask

Is ElevenLabs free?

Yes, with 10,000 credits a month, about ten minutes of speech, and no commercial licence.

How much does ElevenLabs cost?

Starter is $6, Creator $22, Pro $99, Scale $299 and Business $990 a month. Checked 17 November 2026.

Can I use free output commercially?

No. A commercial licence starts on Starter.

Do failed generations use credits?

Yes. Credits are spent on generation, so re-recording costs you twice.

How long is 10,000 credits?

Roughly ten minutes of finished speech.

Is there a yearly discount?

Yes, annual billing is cheaper than monthly on every paid tier.

Verdict

Start on the cheapest tier that covers the limit you actually hit. For most people that is one step up from free, not the top plan, and yearly billing is usually 15 to 20 percent lower.
