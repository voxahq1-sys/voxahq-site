Zapier pricing and free plan limits

Paid plans from $19.99. Prices checked 17 November 2026 against the vendor's pricing page.

Zapier is the automation tool with the most integrations, and the one most non-technical teams start with. The free plan is real but crippled: 100 tasks a month and two-step Zaps only, so any workflow that branches or needs a third step is paid.

What the free plan gives you

  • 100 tasks per month
  • Two-step Zaps only
  • Unlimited Zaps, Tables and Forms
  • Basic access to AI features
The limit that breaks first: the task counter. Every step of every Zap spends a task, so a five-step workflow finishing 200 times is 1,000 tasks, not 200.

What you actually need

  • Trying automation: Free. Two steps is enough for a form-to-sheet test.
  • One or two real workflows: Professional at $19.99 annual for 750 tasks.
  • Branching workflows: Professional, because filters and paths are paid.
  • A team sharing automations: Team seats from $69.

Cheaper alternatives

n8n self-hosted is free and bills per execution rather than per step, which is far cheaper for long workflows. Make sits between the two on price. If you can run a server, n8n wins on cost.

Questions people ask

Is Zapier free?

Yes, with 100 tasks a month and two-step Zaps only.

How much is Zapier Professional?

$19.99 a month billed annually, or $29.99 monthly, for 750 tasks. Checked 17 November 2026.

What is a task?

One successful step in a Zap. A three-step Zap that runs once spends three tasks.

Why can I not build a multi-step Zap?

Multi-step workflows, filters and paths are paid features.

Is Zapier cheaper than n8n?

For a few simple Zaps, yes. For long workflows n8n is cheaper because it bills per run, not per step.

Can I switch later?

Yes, but rebuilding workflows is manual work. Count your tasks before committing.

Verdict

Start on the cheapest tier that covers the limit you actually hit. For most people that is one step up from free, not the top plan, and yearly billing is usually 15 to 20 percent lower.
