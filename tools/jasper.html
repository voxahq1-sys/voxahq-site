Jasper pricing and free plan limits

Paid plans from $59. Prices checked 17 November 2026 against the vendor's pricing page.

Jasper has no free plan. The Pro seat costs $59 a user a month billed yearly, or $69 month to month, and Business seats start at $69 with more brand voices and workflow features.

What the free plan gives you

  • No free plan, trial only
  • Per-seat pricing
  • Cheapest seat is $59/mo yearly, $69 monthly
  • Team seats from $69 per user
The limit that breaks first: the seat model. A three-person team pays three times $59, which is more than three ChatGPT Plus seats that most writers prefer anyway.

What you actually need

  • One writer: a $20 chat assistant does most of this better value.
  • Marketing team with brand rules: Jasper makes sense when consistency across people matters.
  • Evaluating: use the trial, then compare output against Claude at $20 before signing a year.

Cheaper alternatives

Claude at $20 and ChatGPT at $20 both write well for a third of the price. Copy.ai is cheaper to start at $24 a month. Jasper's advantage is brand-voice control for teams, not raw writing quality.

Questions people ask

Is Jasper free?

No. There is only a trial.

How much does Jasper cost?

$59 per user a month billed yearly, or $69 monthly. Checked 17 November 2026.

Is it per user?

Yes, every seat is paid.

Is Jasper better than ChatGPT?

For enforcing one brand voice across a team, it can be. For general writing, a $20 chat plan matches it.

Does it have a yearly discount?

Yes, the $59 price is the annual rate.

Can I cancel?

Yes, at the end of the billing period.

Verdict

Start on the cheapest tier that covers the limit you actually hit. For most people that is one step up from free, not the top plan, and yearly billing is usually 15 to 20 percent lower.
