Copy.ai free plan limits in 2026

What the free tier gives you, the exact point it stops, and the cheapest way past it. Every figure is dated on our Copy.ai pricing page.

The short answer: Copy.ai has no free plan. The entry tier bundles five seats at $24 a month billed yearly, or $29 monthly.

What the free plan gives you

  • No free plan
  • Entry tier bundles five seats
  • Cheapest paid tier is $24 a month yearly, $29 monthly
  • Growth tier above it for workflow volume

The limits that actually stop you

LimitWhat it means
Free planNone

Where it breaks in real use

Copy.ai breaks on the bundle, not the price. Five seats for $24 is cheap per person and expensive for a solo operator who needs one.

How to make the free plan last longer

  • Test the trial on your most repetitive writer task, which is where the templates earn their price.
  • Count the seats you actually need: five are bundled whether you use them or not.
  • If one person does the writing, a $20 assistant is the cheaper route to the same output.
  • Export your prompt library before the trial ends.

What the paid plans cost

$24 a month billed yearly, $29 monthly, five seats included. Growth sits above it for teams running workflow volume.

Who the free plan is actually for

  • Small marketing teams splitting one subscription
  • Anyone producing the same asset type every week
  • Deciding between a template tool and a general assistant

Cheaper ways to get the same work done

A $20 assistant replaces most of the writing use case. If workflow automation is the real need, n8n self-hosted is free.

Verdict: Copy.ai has no free tier, so the real question is whether the entry price beats a $20 assistant doing the same job. For most single users it does not; for a team that needs the templates or the licence, it does.

Questions people ask

Is the free plan enough for a small business?

Usually for one person testing the tool, rarely once the output goes to a client. The limit that decides it is not the message count, it is the licence, the seat count or the file size.

Do the limits change without notice?

Yes. Vendors adjust free tiers quietly, usually by tightening a cap rather than removing a feature. That is why our pricing pages carry the date each figure was read.

Should I upgrade or switch tools?

Upgrade if the tool is where your work already lives and the limit is volume. Switch if the limit is a licence or a seat count, because paying more for the same restriction is the worst of both.
