This Cookie Policy explains how VOXA uses cookies and similar technologies on [WEBSITE URL] (the "Site").

What cookies are

Cookies are small text files stored on your device by your browser. They help websites remember your actions and preferences, keep sessions working, measure traffic and support advertising.

Types of cookies we use

  • Essential cookies. Needed for the Site to function, including security and basic preferences. The Site cannot work properly without them.
  • Analytics cookies. Used to count visits and understand which pages are used, so we can improve the Site. Where required, these are set only with your consent.
  • Advertising cookies. Set by advertising partners, including Google, to show relevant ads and measure their performance. These may track activity across sites.
  • Preference cookies. Remember choices you make, such as language or dismissed notices.

Third-party cookies

Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this Site and other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you. You can opt out of personalised advertising through Google's Ads Settings or at aboutads.info and youronlinechoices.eu.

Managing cookies

You can control or delete cookies through your browser settings. Most browsers also offer a private or incognito mode. Blocking all cookies may affect how the Site works. Where consent is required, you can change or withdraw it at any time.

Do Not Track

Because there is no common industry standard for honouring Do Not Track signals, the Site does not currently respond to them. Where applicable law grants opt-out rights, we honour requests made through the channels described above.

Changes

We may update this policy to reflect changes in technology or law. The revised version will appear on this page with a new date.
